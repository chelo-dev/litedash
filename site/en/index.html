OBD scanner built in

The Workshop: talk to your engine.

Plug an ELM327 adapter into the OBD port (under the steering wheel) over Bluetooth, Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi or USB. No other app needed.

  • Car health: your car seen from above with a dot on the part that needs attention.
  • Trouble codes explained in plain words, and cleared with a double confirmation.
  • Will you pass inspection? The check engine light and the monitors they check.
  • Live charts and sensors, a timed 0–60 and a trip log.
  • Alerts if the engine overheats or the battery isn't charging.
Tire pressure (TPMS)

Your tires, without the maker's app.

Works with the USB-receiver kits sold for Android head units and the most common Bluetooth sensor families. Some cars even report factory tire pressure over OBD.

  • Pressure stuck to each wheel of your 3D car, even while you spin it.
  • Tap your car to see it from above with all four tires.
  • Alerts for low pressure, leaks, heat, sensor battery and lost signal.
  • Pair, swap and rotate sensors with a tap.
Like a real stereo

Music, radio and map on your home screen.

What used to take three apps is already here.

  • USB drive with its own player: folders, artists and albums.
  • Bluetooth and your music app (Spotify, YouTube Music…) with play, next and cover art.
  • FM radio from your head unit in its own widget and, if you want, internet radio with stations near you.
  • Live map, flat or tilted, and the next turn without opening Waze.
  • Split screen: the map on one half and your car on the other.

FAQ

Before you install it.

Which screens does it work on?

Android car and motorcycle head units (the 7", 9", 10" and 11" ones, landscape or portrait), tablets and phones, from Android 6.0. Tested on Android 6 with 1 GB of RAM.

How do I make it my home screen?

Install the APK, open LiteDash and tap "Make it your home". Android asks which home to use: pick LiteDash and "Always".

Is it free? Does it have ads?

Free, ad-free, no accounts and no tracking. The code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 license.

Do I need internet?

Not to use it. Only for what you ask: downloading your 3D car, weather, internet radio, the map or checking for updates.

What do I need for the Workshop and tires?

For the Workshop, an ELM327 adapter (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or USB). For tires, a TPMS kit with a USB receiver or Bluetooth sensors; some cars report them over OBD.

What about Google Play?

Already on Google Play, or grab the APK from GitHub. Same app: it installs and updates the same wherever you download it.
